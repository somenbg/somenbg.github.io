Data platform · Enterprise engineering · ZoomInfo

Enterprise data delivery and analytics platform

Scalable delivery and processing for enterprise analytics and customer-facing data solutions. Implemented while I was a Data Solutions Engineer at ZoomInfo.

Implemented at ZoomInfo

Problem

Customer data delivery was taking more than two days, analytical workloads needed a shared Databricks platform, and a lead-ranking product had to process on the order of a billion records.

What changed

  • A data delivery framework reduced turnaround from more than two days to under two hours.
  • Contributed to enterprise data solutions associated with more than $10 million in revenue.
  • Helped migrate analytical workloads to Databricks for more than 40 analysts.
  • Worked on propensity-based lead ranking at approximately one billion records.
  • Integrated Databricks workflows, APIs, and infrastructure automation into day-to-day processing.

Takeaway

The delivery-time result was a platform change: fewer manual handoffs, and processing that analysts and downstream products could rerun. The revenue, migration, and scale figures describe that body of work. They are not a single benchmark.
