2018 — 2026

A letter

You have been my home in the light.

Gargi, eight years is long enough to know someone when nothing is performing. Not the version of you I met, and not a highlight reel. You, on ordinary days, in the actual light of the room.

You have been there for everything. The wins that felt too big to hold alone. The stretches where I was tired, stubborn, or still learning how to be someone worth coming home to. The logistics. The feelings. The quiet faith that we would sort it out, even when the sorting took longer than either of us wanted.

A lot of love is just staying. You stayed. You turned the staying into a life instead of an endurance test, and that is the thing I am most grateful for. I want it written down where you can see it, in color, not muttered into a pillow.

I love you. Present tense. Not only for what you do for me — though you do plenty — but because the day is simply better with you in it. Mine especially.

For everything

Thank you, in more than one color.

  • The ordinary days

    The unremarkable mornings, the errands, the “what do you want for dinner.” Those are the ones that added up to eight years. Thank you for building a life out of them with me.

  • The heavy days

    You did not only show up when it was easy. You stayed when it cost you something. I noticed. I still notice. Thank you for not flinching when things got real.

  • The version of me that was a lot

    You have loved me through moods, mistakes, and the seasons where I was not my best offer. Thank you for not making me earn the basic fact of you.

  • The laughter

    You make hard things lighter without pretending they are not hard. I want that sound in every room we live in. Thank you for keeping us human.

  • The faith

    You kept believing in us, and in me, on days I had misplaced both. That kind of belief is a shelter. Thank you for holding it when I could not.

  • The next part

    Eight years is not a finish line. Thank you for still wanting a future with me in it. I want one with you in it, too. Obviously. Annoyingly. Completely.

And because I love you

Please get your shit together.

Gargi. My love. I am saying this with my whole chest and a completely unserious amount of side-eye.

I have watched you be steady, funny, brave, and the person who shows up for everyone else. I have also watched you leave your own life in drafts. You already know the list. I am not going to publish it. You do not need a webpage to tell you which things have been sitting in the corner growing teeth.

The plan you keep rewriting. The thing you said you would start. The days that would feel like yours if you stopped negotiating with them. Get the appointment. Send the message. Finish the thing. Stop treating your future like a tab you will get back to when you feel ready. Ready is not coming to collect you.

This is not me standing over you. This is me poking your arm, because eight years has taught me that you rise when someone who loves you refuses to let you hide. I build systems for a living. I cannot build this one for you. I can stand next to you while you do, nag you with love, and refuse to call the pile fine.

The pile is not fine. It is a pile.

I love you too much to watch you keep postponing yourself. So: get your shit together. I will be right here while you do it. I am not going anywhere.

Still yours

Happy anniversary, Gargi.

I love you.
